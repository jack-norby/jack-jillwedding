Our wedding

Our wedding will be, first and foremost, a worship service.

We both follow Jesus, and the day will reflect that. There will be prayer, singing, and Scripture read aloud, and much of what's spoken will be addressed to God before it's addressed to us.

For us, marriage isn't only a commitment between two people — it's a covenant made before God. Scripture describes it as a picture of something greater: Christ as a bridegroom who is coming back for his bride, the Church. When we say our vows, we're pointing to that.

If any of this is unfamiliar to you, you are every bit as welcome. We'd far rather you come as you are and see what we believe than feel you needed to be anything else. We're honored you'll be with us.
