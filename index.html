Our wedding

Our wedding isn't just a celebration of us; it's meant to point to something bigger.

Marriage is the act of two people and two lives becoming one (Genesis 2:24). Even greater than this, marriage points us to the hope in waiting that Jesus is coming back for His bride, the Church (Revelation 19:7-9, 21:2). Because of this, we want our wedding to be a representation of that truth and how God has shown His abundance of grace and love through communion in Him (2 Corinthians 13:14).

Our goal in planning our wedding was to give everyone who attends a little glimpse of God's kingdom and eternity with Him. Jesus has changed both of our lives in so many ways, and we want to be able to share that with our closest friends and family.

If this is not your normal environment, please do not let that keep you home. We are grateful to have you in our lives and cherish your presence. It would mean so much to us to have you there and share this special day with you.
