Our wedding

Before it is anything else, our wedding is a worship service. We both love Jesus, and this day belongs to Him as much as it belongs to us.

So there will be singing, and praying, and Scripture read out loud, and a whole lot of joy. A good deal of what gets said that afternoon will be said to God before it is said to us.

Marriage, to us, is bigger than a promise between two people. It is a covenant made before God, and it is a picture of something greater still: Christ coming back one day for His bride, the Church. Every vow we make is pointing at that.

If church is not your world, please do not let that keep you home. You are so welcome here, exactly as you are. Come, sing if you feel like it, and celebrate with us. More than anything, we hope you leave having felt a little of the warmth and joy that Jesus has brought into our lives.

We cannot wait to have you with us.
