Travel

For those of you coming from out of town — here are a few places to stay, listed by how close they are to the church. We don't have a room block, so book whatever suits you best.

Adel

Right in town — a few minutes from the church

Big Blue Bed and Breakfast

A restored 1890s Victorian in town, with private bathrooms and a full homemade breakfast. Only a handful of rooms, so book early.

Flying in

 

Des Moines International (DSM)

The closest airport, about 35 minutes from the church. Rental cars are available at the airport, and we'd recommend having a car — rideshare out to Adel can be slow.
