FAQs

What should I wear?

Semi-formal attire. The ceremony will be held outdoors, so you may want to keep that in mind when choosing shoes.

Is the ceremony indoors or outdoors?

The ceremony will be held outdoors, and the reception will be indoors.

Will the ceremony and reception be at the same place?

Yes. Both will be held at New Hope Church, so there's no need to travel between the two.

Will there be alcohol?

No. Alcohol will not be served, and it isn't permitted anywhere on the church grounds.

What food will be served?

We'll have a charcuterie board, a coffee cart, and an assortment of snacks and refreshments. We won't be serving a full meal, so we'd suggest having lunch before you join us.

Are kids welcome?

Absolutely. Children are warmly welcome, and we'd love to have your whole family with us. Please reach out if you have any questions.

Can I bring a plus one?

Your invitation lists everyone we've reserved a seat for. If you think we've missed someone, please reach out and let us know.

Where should I park?

Parking is free and available on site at New Hope Church.

Who do I contact with other questions?

Please reach out to either of us anytime.
