FAQs

What should I wear?

Semi-formal. The ceremony is outdoors, so keep that in mind when you are picking out what to wear.

Is the ceremony indoors or outdoors?

The ceremony is outdoors and the reception is inside.

Will the ceremony and reception be at the same place?

Yes, both are at New Hope Church. Once you are there, you are there.

Will there be alcohol?

We will not be serving alcohol, and it is not permitted on the church grounds. There will be very good coffee, though.

What food will be served?

No formal meal, but nobody is going hungry. There will be a generous charcuterie spread, cake, a coffee cart, and plenty to graze on.

Are kids welcome?

Yes! We would love to have your whole family there. Just reach out if you have any questions.

Can I bring a plus one?

Your invitation lists everyone we have saved a seat for. If you think we missed someone, please tell us and we will take a look.

Where should I park?

Free parking, right there at the church.

Who do I contact with other questions?

Reach out to either of us anytime. We are happy to hear from you.
