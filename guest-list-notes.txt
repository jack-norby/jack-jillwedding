HOW LOOKUP WORKS, SO YOU CAN NAME THINGS WELL
-------------------------------------------------------------
A guest finds their household by typing EITHER:
  - the Household label exactly, e.g. "Vos", or
  - any first name in the Members list exactly, e.g. "Tara"

Labels like "Norby (Andrew & Shauna)" still work when someone
types just "Norby" - they'll see a small picker listing both
Norby households and choose theirs. That is why the labels need
to be distinguishable to a guest.

"Fast Eddie" has no surname, so he is his own household and
finds himself by typing "Fast Eddie".
